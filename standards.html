Standards for Theses and Seminar Papers

Official Guidelines • LEO

This sheet contains the main layout and citation requirements for theses and seminar papers at LEO. The presented examples are not complete, and we refer you to the suggested style guide for further information.

Formatting

  • Page margins:
    • 2.5 cm top
    • 2 cm bottom
    • 4 cm left
    • 2 cm right
  • Page numbering:
    • Pages preceding main part: Roman numbers (except cover)
    • Main part: Arabic numbers
  • Font and type size: Times New Roman 12pt or Arial 11pt.
  • Line alignment and spacing: Justified, 1.5 lines.

Citation Guidelines

Citation Style

  • Use a common citation style: APA style.
  • Use indirect citations if the main thought, but not the exact wording of a quotation, is important.
  • Citations are typically not in footnotes.
  • All citations must be verifiable using the reference list. Citations that are not sufficiently marked as such may be considered plagiarism. Plagiarism is an act of academic fraud.

Basic In-Text Citation Examples

Indirect (main thought):

  • Fehr (1999) has argued that sanctions enforce norms.
  • It has been argued that sanctions enforce norms (Fehr, 1999).

Direct (exact wording):

  • Fehr (1999) wrote: “sanctions enforce norms.”

Detailed In-Text Citation Rules & Examples

When citing an author with multiple publications, list the years chronologically for different years, e.g., Konow (2000, 2023) or Fehr and Schmidt (1999, 2006). If an author has multiple works in the same year, append lowercase letters to distinguish them, e.g., Acemoglu and Restrepo (2018a, 2018b) or parenthetically (Acemoglu & Restrepo, 2018a, 2018b).

Narrative Examples (Authors in the sentence):

  • Acemoglu and Restrepo (2018a) argue that artificial intelligence transforms task allocation, whereas Acemoglu and Restrepo (2018b) show how technological progress drives the race between human labor and automation.
  • Over two decades of research, Konow (2000, 2023) consistently examines how cognitive dissonance and moral salience shape allocation decisions and altruistic behavior.

Parenthetical Examples (Citations in parentheses):

  • Recent theoretical models demonstrate that technological advancements shift factor shares and alter labor demand across economic tasks (Acemoglu & Restrepo, 2018a, 2018b).
  • Laboratory experiments demonstrate that risk-taking, accountability, and social norms heavily influence individual views on fair distribution (Cappelen et al., 2013; Fehr & Schmidt, 1999; Konow, 2000).

References Guidelines

  • The list of references includes all references that have been used in the seminar paper or theses. References that have not been used are not included.
  • The formatting should be in a consistent, uniform formatting style.
  • The citation style has guidelines for dealing with various sources such as journal articles, monographs, handbooks, and discussion papers.
